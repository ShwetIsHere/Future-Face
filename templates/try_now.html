📸 Recommended Photo Angles

Face Angle & Quality Guide

To avoid distorted, messy, or unnatural AI aging results, please follow these portrait angle recommendations:

Permitted Angle Range (-45° to +45° Horiz, -16° to +45° Vert) Front & semi-profile portraits with visible eyes & nose contours
Frontal Portrait (0°–15°) Recommended for optimal identity preservation & natural aging

✓ What Works Best

  • Front to Semi-Profile: Face camera with horizontal turn within -45° to +45°.
  • Vertical Angle: Keep vertical tilt between -16° (slight chin-up) and +45° (chin-down).
  • Close & Centered: Your face should fill 30–70% of the photo frame.
  • Clear & Focused: Good lighting without heavy motion blur.

✕ What to Avoid

  • Extreme Profile (>45°): Avoid full side profile (>45°) that hides half the face.
  • Steep Chin-Up (<-30°): Avoid steep under-the-chin perspective beyond -30°.
  • Sideways Tilt (>10°): Keep head straight without leaning sideways past ±10°.
  • Distant or Blurry Photos: Tiny or unfocused faces lead to poor feature resolution.
